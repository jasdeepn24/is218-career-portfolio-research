# Career & Portfolio Lab

NJIT IS218 · Career research and portfolio planning · November 11, 2026

A small research notebook connecting employer expectations to Business & Information Systems coursework and verifiable project evidence.

## Explore the lab

- [Job Research](./job-research.md): four official employer postings, qualifications, locations, and availability caveats.
- [Career Comparison](./career-comparison.md): two possible directions, realistic entry routes, and learning gaps.
- [Portfolio Plan](./portfolio-plan.md): an audience, design options, ethical choices, and testable user stories for a future portfolio.

## Evidence rules

Employer facts are paraphrased from linked official sources. Interpretations and future work are labeled as proposals. A visible job description is not proof that applications remain open.

Course status was checked against a student-supplied unofficial transcript. Project details also use the student's own account, the public RPS repository, and the existing local highway research documentation. Coursework does not establish professional mastery.

**Current work:** Fall 2026 courses are in progress. RPS is completed according to the student. Campus Help and the highway game are planning work; the highway project is not a playable game.

## Privacy and scope

This is research and planning, not a finished professional portfolio. Personal career reflection, the raw transcript, grades, and sensitive personal details are excluded. No application, push, or publication is part of this work.
